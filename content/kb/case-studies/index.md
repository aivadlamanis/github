## Route Questions Here When

- Has NexaFlow helped a company in a given industry, and what results did it get?
- Which proof points support faster onboarding, fewer errors, hours saved or reduced manual work?

- Which customer used a given system (Salesforce, NetSuite, SAP, Epic, Shopify)?
