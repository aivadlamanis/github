## Version Conflicts and Precedence

- List prices: pricing_guide_v3.md (NEXA-020) is the single source of truth and wins over the historical Pricing Guide v2 figures in pricing_faq.md (NEXA-019) and the historical note in enterprise_pricing.md (NEXA-011). The v2 figures were superseded on 1 July 2026 and must never be quoted as current.
- Approval authority: the tier table in enterprise_pricing.md describes the floor as the lowest price a Sales Manager can approve, with anything lower needing VP Sales approval. The approval matrix in discount_policy.md has the Sales Manager approving discounts of 10.1 to 20% and the VP Sales 20.1 to 30%. Winner: discount_policy.md for approval authority, which enterprise_pricing.md itself points to. Flagged for human review.
